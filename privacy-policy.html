Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This page describes what happens when you use the enquiry form on rajendrasinghpardeshi.com.

1. Information in the Email Draft

The form is handled in your browser and opens a prefilled email draft using your email application. The form does not send information to this website or store it in a website database. Information is sent only if you choose to send the email. Avoid including sensitive medical details or emergency information.

2. Optional Messaging Consent

The enquiry form includes this optional checkbox: “Optional: I agree to be contacted about this enquiry by SMS, RCS, or WhatsApp.” Selecting it indicates consent to those messaging channels for this enquiry. Leaving it unchecked does not indicate messaging consent. The checkbox is not required to prepare the email draft.

You may withdraw this consent for future messages by contacting the practice using the details below.

3. How We Use Collected Information

  • To prepare an email draft for you to review and send.
  • If you send the email, to respond to the enquiry using the contact details you provide.

4. Data Disclosure & Service Providers

The website itself does not transmit the form details. If you send the prepared email, your email provider and the recipient’s email systems will handle it. This site links to third-party services; those services have their own privacy practices.

5. Retention and Security

This website does not set a retention period for enquiry emails. Retention and security depend on the email systems used by you and the recipient. Website hosting logs and any associated retention practices should be confirmed with the hosting provider.

6. Your Rights

For an email enquiry you sent, contact the recipient using the details below to discuss access, correction, or deletion. The applicable email providers may also hold copies under their own practices.
