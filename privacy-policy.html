Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dr. Rajendrasingh Pardeshi (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal information collected via rajendrasinghpardeshi.com.

1. Information We May Collect

When you use the consultation or contact forms on our website, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, and the specific nature of your clinical enquiry. Please note: Do not submit highly sensitive emergency details, comprehensive patient medical histories, diagnostic scans, or financial data through this general inquiry form.

2. Communication & Messaging Consent (RCS / SMS / WhatsApp)

By submitting your details via this portal, you authorize Dr. Rajendrasingh Pardeshi and our authorized clinical administrative team to contact you via Telephone, SMS, Rich Communication Services (RCS), WhatsApp, and Email regarding your enquiry or requested consultation. This communication consent overrides any National Do Not Call (NDNC/DND) registry entry as per applicable Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) regulations for requested healthcare coordination.

You may withdraw this communication consent at any time by contacting us directly using the details provided below.

3. How We Use Collected Information

  • To respond to your healthcare inquiries and schedule consultation appointments.
  • To coordinate follow-ups related to the Menopause & Osteoporosis Clinic initiative.
  • To ensure portal security and protect against fraudulent submissions.
  • To comply with applicable regional and national healthcare records standards.

4. Data Disclosure & Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information may be processed by trusted infrastructure service providers that support website hosting, domain email delivery, and communication gateways, or disclosed where mandated by Indian law or court orders.

5. Data Retention & Security Measures

We retain enquiry details only for as long as necessary to fulfill the medical coordination purpose or comply with statutory record-keeping. We implement industry-standard organizational and technical safeguards, although no internet transmission can be guaranteed completely immune from unauthorized breach.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data you have submitted, subject to applicable medical compliance retention obligations.
